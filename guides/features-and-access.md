# Roles, Workspaces, and Feature Availability

ClubTrack does not show every page to every user. What you can see depends on your account role, your club's enabled features/add-ons, and some club-specific permissions.

## Member role

Members primarily use the **Member Workspace**.

Typical member pages include:

- Dashboard
- My Requests
- Profile & Licences
- Invoices & Renewal
- Security & Kiosk PIN
- Linked Juniors
- Record Participation
- Participation History
- My Firearms
- Membership Card, when enabled
- My Documents, when enabled
- Newsletters, when enabled

## Range Officer role

Range officers use selected operational screens in the **Admin Workspace** rather than receiving full administrator access.

The current navigation provides range officers with access to operational workflows such as the **Participation Queue**. AssetTrack Bookings can also be available when the add-on is enabled and the club permits the Range Officer role.

## Admin role

Administrators can use both the **Admin Workspace** and their own **Member Workspace**.

The Admin Workspace includes club-management and compliance functions such as member management, participation processing, financials, club settings, exports, and audit tools.

## NORI Workspace

The **NORI Workspace** is separate from the normal Member and Admin workspaces.

It appears only when:

- the club's NORI add-on is enabled; and
- the signed-in account has permission to access the workspace.

The workspace can include the NORI Directory and Membership Applications.

## Feature-controlled pages

Some menu items are controlled by club feature settings.

### Membership Cards

When **Membership Cards** is disabled, members do not see their Membership Card page and administrators do not see related membership-card tools.

### Document Uploads

When **Document Uploads** is disabled, member and administrator document-management pages are unavailable.

### Newsletters

When **Newsletters** is disabled, newsletter menu items are hidden.

## Add-on controlled pages

Some functionality is provided as an add-on rather than a standard feature flag.

### AssetTrack

When the AssetTrack/asset-management add-on is enabled, authorised users can see:

- **AssetTrack Manage**
- **AssetTrack Bookings**, subject to booking settings and allowed roles

### NORI

When the NORI add-on is enabled, eligible users may receive the NORI Workspace.

## Why a menu item may be missing

If a user cannot see a page, check these in order:

1. **Role** — does the member account have the correct role?
2. **Workspace** — are they currently in Member, Admin, or NORI Workspace?
3. **Feature flag** — is the feature enabled under Club Settings?
4. **Add-on** — is the required add-on active for the club?
5. **Club permission** — does the feature have additional allowed-role settings?
6. **Session** — log out and sign in again after a role or access change.

A missing menu item is often expected configuration behaviour rather than a browser problem.

## Pinned navigation

ClubTrack's navigation supports pinned/favourite items for eligible pages. A pinned item is still subject to role and feature permissions: pinning a page does not grant access to it.

## Support guidance

When reporting an access problem, provide:

- club name or tenant
- member number
- account role
- expected workspace/page
- whether other users with the same role can see the page

Never send a password, kiosk PIN, verification code, or kiosk QR code.
