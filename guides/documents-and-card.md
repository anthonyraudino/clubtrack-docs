# Documents and Membership Card

ClubTrack can provide member document storage and a digital membership card. These features only appear when your club has enabled them.

## Upload a document

Go to **Documents → My Documents**.

1. Select the document type.
2. If applicable, choose whether the file is the front or back of the document.
3. Choose the file from your device.
4. Select **Upload document**.

Supported uploads in the current web app are:

- JPEG/JPG images
- PNG images
- PDF files

Current upload limits are:

- images: **1 MB maximum per file**
- PDFs: **5 MB maximum per file**

The available document types include driver's licence, Medicare, longarm, handgun, other, and — when membership cards are enabled — membership-card profile photo.

## Profile photos

If membership cards are enabled, you can upload a profile photo from **My Documents**.

Use a clear, front-facing portrait. Depending on your club's settings, the new photo may either become active immediately or remain **Pending approval** until an administrator reviews it.

Possible photo statuses include:

- **Approved** — currently available for member-facing surfaces and the membership card
- **Pending approval** — uploaded but not yet approved
- **Rejected** — not approved; the administrator may provide a reason

If a new photo is pending approval, an existing approved photo may continue to be used until the new one is accepted.

## Download or remove a document

Your document list contains the files currently attached to your member account.

Use the available actions to download a file or delete it. Deleting a document cannot be undone, so make sure you still have your own copy if you may need it later.

## Digital membership card

If enabled, go to **Documents → Membership Card**.

Your club controls the design and information displayed on the card. Depending on club settings, it may include:

- your member details
- membership tier
- profile photo
- qualifications
- kiosk QR code

The page can be used to view and print your digital membership card.

## Kiosk QR code and privacy

A kiosk sign-in QR code may also be available from **My Account → Security & Kiosk PIN** after you set a kiosk PIN.

Treat kiosk QR codes as credentials. The security screen warns that the QR contains your member number and PIN, so do not post or share it publicly.

## I cannot see Documents or Membership Card

These items are feature-controlled by your club. If an expected menu item is missing, contact a club administrator to confirm whether the feature is enabled for your club.
