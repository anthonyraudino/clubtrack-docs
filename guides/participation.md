# Recording and Reviewing Participation

ClubTrack can record matches, practice sessions, and other club activities against your membership. Your club controls which disciplines, matches, participation roles, and entry methods are available.

## Record a participation from the Member Workspace

Go to **Records → Record Participation**.

> If your club has disabled remote participation recording, ClubTrack will tell you to use kiosk mode or contact an administrator instead.

### 1. Choose the activity

Select:

1. **Discipline**
2. **Match or activity**
3. **Participating as** — for example, competitor or another club-configured role

Only entries enabled by your club are shown.

### 2. Add equipment and performance information

You can optionally select the firearm used and enter a score.

For handgun activities, ClubTrack can associate an LRD handgun class with the participation. The class may come from the selected match or from the firearm recorded in **My Firearms**.

If the handgun class on your selected firearm does not match the class configured for the selected match, ClubTrack displays a warning before allowing you to continue. Check the details carefully before choosing to record the entry anyway.

## 3. Set the date and location

Select the participation date. Self-recorded entries support today or a past date; future dates cannot be selected.

For an activity at another club, enable **Away match** and enter the host club name.

An away match can also include optional supporting information:

- a PractiScore or other results URL
- an image or PDF evidence file

Providing evidence can make it easier for a club administrator to verify an away-club participation.

## 4. Add notes

Use **Comments** for any useful context about the participation. This is optional.

## 5. Submit the participation

Review the Session Summary and select **Record Participation**.

A newly self-recorded entry may appear as **Pending** while it waits for administrator approval, depending on the club workflow.

## Review your participation history

Go to **Records → Participation History** to view and filter your recorded participation entries.

Your dashboard may also show participation statistics, recent activity, and annual participation progress.

## Annual handgun participation progress

For clubs using Victorian handgun participation compliance features, the dashboard can show progress such as:

- approved events
- match events
- competitor events
- separate participation days
- relevant handgun-class requirements

The class-specific calculation depends on accurate information in **Records → My Firearms**. If your handgun is missing or has the wrong class, the dashboard may not be able to calculate your class requirements correctly.

Compliance information in ClubTrack is a tracking aid. If you are unsure whether a participation satisfies a legal or regulatory requirement, confirm it with your club and the relevant regulator.

## Why is my participation still pending?

An administrator or range officer may need to review the entry first. ClubTrack's Participation Queue can be used to approve or reject submitted records.

For an away match, make sure the host club and any available results/evidence are complete. If an entry is rejected, contact your club if you need clarification or correction.

## I cannot see the match I need

The list is controlled by the club's Match Setup configuration. If a discipline or match is not available:

1. confirm you selected the correct discipline;
2. reset the form and try again;
3. contact a club administrator if the match is still missing.

Administrators can enable approved matches or create club-specific participation entries in Club Settings.
