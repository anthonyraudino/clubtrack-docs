## Feature availability

Some documentation describes features that your club may not use. Membership cards, document uploads, newsletters, AssetTrack, NORI, guest kiosk sign-in, card payments, and other features can be enabled or disabled by club configuration.

If a menu item mentioned in a guide does not appear, first check [Roles, Workspaces, and Feature Availability](/guides/features-and-access).

## Getting support

For a support request, include your member number, club/tenant, device/browser, the page involved, what you were trying to do, and the exact error message where possible.

Do not send passwords, kiosk PINs, login verification codes, kiosk QR codes, or full payment-card details.
